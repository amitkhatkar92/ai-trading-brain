## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The 0.25% risk rule is correctly implemented.  
It is applied to the **strategy budget** — not to TOTAL_CAPITAL.

The strategy budget itself is the product of three nested multipliers:
`TOTAL_CAPITAL × regime_exposure × strategy_share`

At ₹10,000 capital with bull_trend regime and the default strategy share, this chain
produces an effective risk of **₹2 per trade** — not ₹25.

No code is wrong. No configuration value is incorrect for its design intent.
The system was designed and calibrated for ₹1,00,00,000 (₹1 crore) capital.
At ₹10,000 (1/1000th scale), the risk budget becomes proportionally small.

**Classification: A + D**  
**A** — The nested allocation formula is intentional institutional design.  
**D** — Configuration interaction: `TOTAL_CAPITAL = ₹10,000` is below the design operating point.

## 3. WHERE DOES ₹25 ACTUALLY APPEAR?

**₹25 would require risk to be calculated as:**
```
risk_amount = TOTAL_CAPITAL × MAX_RISK_PER_TRADE_PCT
            = ₹10,000 × 0.0025
            = ₹25
```

The system does NOT use this formula. It uses:
```
risk_amount = strategy_budget × MAX_RISK_PER_TRADE_PCT
            = (TOTAL_CAPITAL × regime_exposure × strategy_share) × MAX_RISK_PER_TRADE_PCT
```

**₹25 at the 0.25% rule is only the theoretical ceiling** — it would only be reached if:
- All capital is deployed in a single strategy (100% share) AND
- No regime reduction is applied (100% deployable)

Neither of those conditions is ever intended to be true simultaneously.
