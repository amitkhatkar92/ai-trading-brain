## Methodology

Two simulated entry scenarios compared on the same universe (211 NSE symbols,
2021–2025, ~256K trading days). Both scenarios use the same exit: day's CLOSE.

| Window | Entry Price | Proxy For |
|---|---|---|
| **OPENING_WINDOW** | OPEN | 09:10–09:30 IST execution |
| **POST_GOVERNANCE** | (O + H + L) ÷ 3 | 09:45+ intraday average |

- **MFE** = (HIGH − entry) / entry × 100  (best intraday point reached)
- **MAE** = max(0, (entry − LOW) / entry × 100)  (worst intraday drawdown)
- **WIN** = CLOSE > entry
- **PF** = Σ(positive PnL %) / |Σ(negative PnL %)|
